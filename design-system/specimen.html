Bloomgard design language

Jaali. One lattice, every product.

A strict structure that lets light through. The tokens, type, motifs and components that make every Bloomgard surface recognisably ours, from the website to the CRM.

Register 01

Editorial

Websites, launches, decks, email. Serif display, kora paper, one mandala, wide space.

Register 02

Product

Apps people work in all day. Geist at 13–14px, dense layouts, light and dark, motifs at a whisper.

Colour

Eight colours from Indian materials.

The brand palette never changes. Interfaces use the role tokens beneath it, which switch between the Kora (light) and Raat (dark) themes.

Product identity

A glyph for every product.

Each product gets a line mark on a 120-unit grid, drawn from Indian geometry. On its app icon the glyph sits in kora on neel, with one marigold dot.

Motifs

Atmosphere, used once.

One large motif per view. In product screens they appear only on empty states, sign-in and onboarding.

Mandala

Ambient art behind heroes and feature bands, 10–14% ink, partly off-canvas. May turn once every 120 seconds.

Lattice
Jaali

A 56px lattice tile over dark bands, footers and dark-theme sidebars. Shown here enlarged; in use it sits at 6–7%, felt rather than seen.

Glow

One marigold-to-sindoor blur behind an editorial hero. Never in product screens.

Components

Plain classes, any stack.

Every component is a CSS class in jaali.css, so it works in React, Vue, Svelte, server templates or plain HTML.
